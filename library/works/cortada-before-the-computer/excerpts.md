# Excerpts — Before the Computer

*Cortada, James W., 1993. Princeton University Press.*

## Overview

Cortada covers the pre-digital data processing industry — the companies and
technologies that handled business information before electronic computers.
Remington Rand occupies a substantial portion of the book, covering its evolution
from Remington Typewriter through the Rand Kardex merger to the Univac era.
The book is the authoritative secondary source on Remington Rand's product
history, including the SOUNDEX, VARIADEX, and KARDEX product lines that are the
core of the information-systems project.

Directly relevant: Cortada documents Remington Rand's filing and indexing product
lines in a corporate history context, naming specific products, acquisition dates,
and market positions. This is the secondary-source grounding that connects the
primary filing records (USPTO patents and trademarks) to established historical
scholarship.
