## Overview

Austrian's biography of Herman Hollerith covers the invention of the punched-card tabulating machine and the founding of what became IBM. It is relevant to the information-systems project primarily as context for the Library Bureau — Hollerith's company eventually merged into the Bureau of the Census apparatus, and the Library Bureau's card-index systems developed alongside and adjacent to the tabulating machine world. Austrian documents the pre-1900 environment in which systematic card filing first emerged as an industrial practice.

Use this reference for:
- The pre-1900 Library Bureau and card-catalog origins
- The distinction between card-filing systems (this project) and tabulating machines (adjacent, not in scope)
- Context for the SOUNDEX citation chain: US495147A and related Library Bureau patents cited in the Russell phonetic indexing patent
