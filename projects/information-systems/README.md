# Information Systems

Research project documenting pre-computer information systems — filing, indexing, visible records, tabulating — through the combined USPTO trademark and patent record.

## Scope

American commercial information systems, roughly 1870–1960. Marks are drawn from the 1900–1939 USPTO trademark dataset. Patent cross-references use the PatentsView API and Google Patents.

## Outputs

| Folder | Contents |
|---|---|
| `output/filing-systems/` | 40 design marks for filing and records organization systems |
| `output/stationery-marks/` | 55 design marks for stationery and writing paper goods |
| `output/soundex-marks/` | SOUNDEX marks (Rand Kardex Bureau, 1927) with patent PDFs and background essay |
