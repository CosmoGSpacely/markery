# animal-marks-1930

Early American technology company trademarks featuring animal imagery, filed 1930 or earlier.

**Research question:** What does an animal communicate in a technology brand — and what does the choice reveal about how a company positioned its products?

**Entities:** Mack Trucks (bulldog), Pratt & Whitney Aircraft (eagle), Eagle Electric Manufacturing (eagle), Pathé Cinema (rooster), Albert Setzer MULE (mule)
