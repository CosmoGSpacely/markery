This project cross-references the U.S. trademarks and patents of American precision-tool
makers — L.S. Starrett, Brown & Sharpe, Snap-on, and Illinois Tool Works — in measuring
instruments (CPC G01B) across 1910–1940.

[[media:illustration-of-a-combination-square]]

The combination square shown above is representative of the period's machinist measuring
tools, the category around which these firms built their marks and patents.
