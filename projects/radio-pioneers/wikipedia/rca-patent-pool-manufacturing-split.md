## Background

The Radio Corporation of America Wikipedia article describes the 1919–1922 patent pool arrangement among GE, Westinghouse, AT&T, and RCA. The article currently lacks a primary-source citation for the specific manufacturing allocation agreed between GE and Westinghouse (60%/40% in RCA's favour of GE). Gleason Archer's *Big Business and Radio* (1939) — written with access to RCA's internal records — is the authoritative source for these specifics.

The relevant passage (p. 9):

> "Since the General Electric Company had a larger volume of wireless business than its rival, the parties agreed that RCA would purchase for resale 60% of its needs from the General Electric Company and 40% from the Westinghouse Company."
