## The Invention

The patent US1261167A, granted 1918-04-02 to Remington Typewriter Company, describes a phonetic indexing system. This system encodes surnames by consonant sound into a letter-number code. It enables retrieval of records without exact spelling and is known as the Soundex system.

## The Mark

The trademark SOUNDEX was filed on 1927-03-31 by RAND KARDEX BUREAU, INC. It designates blank and partially-printed cards and forms for indexes, specifically including index guide cards and separator cards of the type used for phonetic indexes. The registration number 0230958 covers frames and holders for such index cards as well.

## The Connection

The patent US1261167A underlies the SOUNDEX trademarked product by providing the technical method for phonetic indexing.
