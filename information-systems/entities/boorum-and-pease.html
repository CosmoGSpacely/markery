Boorum & Pease

Historical Identity

Boorum & Pease Company of New York was one of the leading manufacturers of blank books in the American market during the late nineteenth and early twentieth centuries. Blank books — ruled ledgers, account books, journals, day books, and related record-keeping volumes — were the primary medium for business accounting and correspondence before the widespread adoption of loose-leaf and card-index systems. Boorum & Pease occupied this market alongside other major producers such as Wilson Jones (which later expanded into filing appliances) and operated in the same commercial space as the companies more directly represented in the information-systems project.

The company registered three trademarks in the 1900–1939 period: "B & P" (serial 71079898, filed 1914), STANDARD B&P (serial 71151746, filed 1921), and BULLDOG (serial 71201487, filed 1924). The marks span a decade and document the company's brand-building across its product line. Boorum & Pease was later acquired by Esselte, the Swedish office-products group, and the brand persists in the Esselte portfolio.

In the Filing Record

Boorum & Pease holds 38 patents in the filing-appliance and blank-book classes covered by this project. The patent record is concentrated in blank-book construction — binding mechanisms, ruling systems, and paper grades — rather than the card-index and visible-record technology that dominates the Remington Rand and Wilson Jones portfolios. This reflects the company's position in the blank-book segment of the office-records market rather than the filing-appliance segment proper.

The three trademarks cover a ten-year span from 1914 to 1924. The "B & P" mark (registration 0112516) is the earliest, providing a house-mark covering the company's full line. STANDARD B&P (registration 0151869) expanded the portfolio with a quality designation seven years later. BULLDOG (registration 0191540) in 1924 added a product-name mark — the bulldog as a metaphor for a binding that grips and holds, a common advertising concept in the fastener and binding trades of the period.

Research Significance

Boorum & Pease is included in the project's entity registry as a representative of the blank-book manufacturing sector — the segment of the office-records trade adjacent to, but distinct from, the filing-appliance and visible-record categories that are the project's primary focus. No confirmed patent-trademark pairs have been identified for Boorum & Pease in the research period. The company's patents are in blank-book construction; the trademarks are house marks and quality designations rather than product-mechanism names. The filing record does not show the compressed patent-then-trademark timing that characterizes the confirmed pairs at Remington Rand, Wilson Jones, and Yawman & Erbe.

The absence of a confirmed pair for Boorum & Pease is itself informative. The company held patents and registered trademarks across the same period as the project's paired entities, but its product strategy centered on brand identity and material quality rather than the mechanism-specific product naming that generates patent-trademark correspondence. BULLDOG sells a binding concept; the Remington Rand and Wilson Jones marks name specific mechanical systems whose technical basis appears in contemporaneous patents.
